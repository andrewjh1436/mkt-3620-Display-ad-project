Campaign description

Targeting, goal, hook, and the benchmark estimates behind the budget.

GOAL

Drive rental requests

A direct-response campaign: every ad and the landing page push one action — submit a rental request.

TARGET AUDIENCE

Iowa State visitors and families

Parents, alumni, and fans traveling to Ames for games, move-in, and campus visits. Secondary: Ames locals who need a car for a few days and students renting in their own name.

PLACEMENT TARGETING

Local and travel sites

Iowa State Daily, Ames Tribune, Cyclones.com, KCCI, WHO 13, Visit Ames, and Tripadvisor — Iowa State and central-Iowa news plus travel and trip-planning sites, geo-targeted to the Ames area. Estimated CPMs run $5 to $14.

THE HOOK

“Need wheels in Ames?”

A short, local question, varied slightly by ad size (“Rent local in Ames.”). Core message: affordable local rentals, easy to request, with rides to DSM Airport.

Financial estimates (4-week campaign)

Modeled on published display benchmarks for travel / vehicle rental. Bought on a CPM basis: impressions lead to clicks, clicks lead to rental requests.

Metric Value
Budget (4 weeks) $1,500
Average CPM (range $5–$14) $8.50
Estimated impressions ~176,000
CTR (display benchmark) 0.52%
Estimated clicks ~918
Conversion rate (display benchmark) 0.50%
Estimated rental requests 4–5

Benchmarks: all-industry Google Display Network averages (CTR ≈ 0.46–0.52%, conversion ≈ 0.5–0.57%, per WordStream); premium local-news and travel placements modeled at an $8.50 blended CPM ($5–$14 range). Figures are planning estimates, not guarantees.

Creative notes

All four sizes share the orange brand block, the same call to action, and the same URL. Wording varies slightly by size so the same message fits each space. Each ad is clickable and leads to the landing page. Car images are illustrations, not photos of the fleet.
